**本篇未完成全文阅读总结。** 该文直接研究人机协作评测，与研究方向中interaction方向相关。提供明确的协作质量评估维度和场景模板，具有方法借鉴价值。但核心贡献是评测benchmark设计，非理论机制研究；协作维度定义偏向任务导向而非心理/认知机制；script user的建模细节有限。证据支持存在评测框架和实验结果，但迁移到行为研究需补充心理状态建模细节。

原始摘要：Command-line interface (CLI) agents increasingly execute longer-horizon tasks, yet existing benchmarks either assume that user instructions remain fixed throughout execution or evaluate task outcomes without capturing runtime collaboration quality. In this paper, we present REALCLI, a paired benchmark that unifies the evaluation of task execution and human-agent collaboration quality on realistic CLI tasks. To comprehensively measure collaboration, REALCLI builds a five-dimensional framework, operationalized into 22 sub-dimensions and 51 interaction scenario templates. REALCLI provides two modes for paired comparison: REALCLI-EXEC supplies complete specifications upfront, whereas REALCLI-COLLAB introduces a scripted user that delivers progressive information, corrections, constraint revisions, and authorization decisions. Evaluations reveal profound capability gaps in frontier agents: the best configuration attains an average score of only 49.2%, and when evaluated by the joint collaboration score ($J_{\mathrm{collab}}$), the peak performance reaches merely 37.6%. These results show that task effectiveness diverges sharply from collaboration quality, with models consistently struggling in steerability, trust calibration, and safety boundary management. REALCLI provides a rigorous, reproducible foundation for measuring and advancing collaborative CLI agents.

## 16. Cultural (In)Competence: Investigating Culture-Specific Stereotype Detection Capabilities of LLMs

等级 **C** · 全文分析未完成 · [论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22545v1)

**本篇未完成全文阅读总结。** 论文评估了LLMs处理文化相关社会认知任务的能力，发现非英语提示和指令微调能改善文化语境考虑。虽非直接构建社会模拟，但提供了文化敏感性建模的技术借鉴路线：选择合适的语言提示和微调策略可使模型更好地捕捉文化语境下的社会认知现象（如刻板印象判断）。

原始摘要：Stereotypes are subjective constructs that are mediated by the cultural context in which they are viewed. Successfully modeling them thus also requires cultural competence. When using large language models (LLMs) for stereotype-related tasks, such as their detection, it is therefore crucial that models can access relevant cultural knowledge and adjust responses accordingly. Since LLMs have shown disparities in what cultural knowledge is available to them, their capacity regarding stereotypes may also be disparate across languages and cultural contexts (e.g., for non-English languages or for non-US contexts). In this paper, we systematically investigate the effectiveness of LLMs for culture-specific stereotype detection across three factors: (1) the prompting language, (2) the instruction tuning language, and (3) the fine-tuning dataset size. We conduct experiments for US, Dutch, and Chinese cultural contexts. Our results suggest that LLMs do not reliably account for cultural contexts and overpredict the presence of stereotypes. Non-English prompts seem to improve consideration of cultural context and thus lead to more effective predictions. Moreover, we find that additional instruction tuning reduces false-positive rates and that training data size moderates detection effectiveness non-monotonically.

## 17. Who Do Perspectivist Models Predict Well? Annotator Agreement, Sparsity, and Performance

原始摘要：Successful completion of perspectivist natural language processing (NLP) annotation tasks requires algorithms to be aligned across diverse perspectives in order to predict how different humans will rate text. This work studies how the performance of such algorithms varies for annotators who behave differently than others, either by providing annotations that diverge from others' responses or by providing fewer annotations altogether. To aid with characterizing model performance, we curate the largest collection to date of perspectivist NLP datasets with ordinal annotations. After evaluating multiple classes of perspectivist algorithms across each of the datasets, we model the relationship between individual annotator idiosyncrasy and algorithm performance. In general, we find that algorithms perform better on annotators who tend to agree with others, but that annotator response rate does not have a statistically significant positive correlation with algorithm performance.

## 18. From Memory Hits to Answerable Evidence: Query-Conditioned Memory Compilation for Long-Term Agent Memory

**本篇未完成全文阅读总结。** 论文明确解决智能体长期记忆中的跨会话证据检索问题，包含记忆定位、归一化边界和证据预算分配机制。虽主要用于对话助手的问答任务，但Query-conditioned compilation机制可迁移用于个体历史对当前决策影响的建模。Memory addressing与evidence presentation解耦的设计思路对模拟个体如何从碎片化历史中构建连贯自我认知具有参考价值。标注uncertain=true因原论文未验证该迁移路径，且LoCoMo和LongMemEval为技术评测基准而非心理状态评估

原始摘要：Long-term agent memory requires recovering answer-relevant evidence dispersed across turns and sessions. Existing systems increasingly use structured memories and adaptive retrieval, yet often pass retrieved or expanded units directly to the reader, coupling memory addressing with evidence presentation and leaving the final context redundant or incomplete. To address this gap, we propose MemCompile, a query-conditioned evidence compilation framework for long-term conversational memory. Given a query, the Address Plane locates relevant support through compact source-linked descriptors and capacity-aware Cells, while a normalization boundary resolves heterogeneous retrieval hits to stable SourcePacket identities. Its Evidence Plane then allocates a bounded evidence budget across complementary packets, admits limited additional support, and renders the retained claims associated with each selected packet into an answer-ready context. MemCompile achieves 80.52% Judge Accuracy on LoCoMo and extends the empirical quality-cost Pareto frontier to a higher-quality regime. Its strong performance on LongMemEval-S further suggests that the benefits of query-conditioned compilation generalize across long-term conversational-memory benchmarks.
