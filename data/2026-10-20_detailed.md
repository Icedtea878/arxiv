原始摘要：The Prisoner's Dilemma has long served as a foundational framework for studying human decision-making and cooperative outcomes across social scenarios, yet human behavior consistently deviates from predictions of fully rational economic theory. Previous works have studied human and LLM outcomes in the Prisoner's Dilemma; however, these studies have lacked at least one key feature: a memory component for LLM agents, accessible open-source models, or comparison against empirical human data. This project investigates whether open-source LLMs, TII Falcon 7B Instruct, Google Gemma 7B, Meta Llama 3.1 8B Instruct, Microsoft Phi Mini MoE Instruct, and Qwen 2.5 7B Instruct, can model human cooperation patterns in repeated Prisoner's Dilemma games, while examining how an LLM's knowledge that its opponent will be shuffled affects its decisions. 原始摘要：Multi-agent LLM systems exchange messages before acting through tools, but message delivery, signing authority, and tool-side enforcement are separate trust boundaries. Controlled paired hidden-state studies reveal four distinctions. 原始摘要：Can one sentence manipulate an agent's belief by targeting a single implicit warrant linking evidence to a conclusion? In this paper, we introduce the one-relation attack (ORA), a novel cognitive attack on this inferential relation. ORA localizes a critical implicit warrant and performs a one-relation intervention using a single source-grounded sentence that qualifies this warrant and steers the agent toward an incorrect belief. Across eighteen models on biomedical inference and vulnerability detection, best-of-$10 attacks overturn initially correct answers at mean rates of 54.9% for proprietary and 80.0% for open-weight models.
