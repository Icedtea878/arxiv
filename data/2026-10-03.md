Motivation: 当前大语言模型在组合推理任务上表现不足，缺乏有效的中间推理过程支撑最终答案，且现有方法往往需要特定任务的标注数据或提示设计。

Method: ReHoPER采用迭代式规划策略：(1) 规划多个候选中间问题；(2) 选择其中一个进行回答；(3) 基于更新后的历史信息重规划。该方法使用通用指令，不依赖标注数据或任务特定设计。

Result: 在多个数据集上优于强基线方法，在组合性最强的设置中提升最为显著。文中还提出了iLLC，一个新的组合推理受控基准数据集。

Conclusion: ReHoPER具有任务无关性和零样本特性，通用性强，可跨数据集和模型迁移，无需微调或任务特定适配，为组合推理提供了一种简单有效的解决方案。

Abstract: We propose ReHoPER, an inference-only, zero-shot method that improves large language models' reasoning by generating and answering intermediate questions along multiple paths before the final answer. It iteratively plans a horizon of candidate intermediate questions, selects one to answer, and replans from the updated history. ReHoPER is task-agnostic, using the same generic instructions across datasets and models without labeled data or task-specific prompt design. Across multiple datasets, including iLLC, a new controlled benchmark for compositional reasoning, ReHoPER outperforms strong baselines, with the largest gains in the most compositional settings. Our implementation and the iLLC generator are publicly available to support future work.
