## 16. STRATA: Self-Maintaining Structured Experience Graphs for Self-Evolving Language Agents

They never forget or deduplicate, and are prone to negative transfer, where a superficially similar but directionally opposite experience is recalled and its concrete steps are copied verbatim, actively harming the agent. ## 18. HACHIMI-II: From Role-Play to Learner Behavior Modeling for Faithful Student Simulation
